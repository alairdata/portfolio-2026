Machine Learning · Healthcare
Cancer Type Prediction
via Gene Expression

A multi-class classification pipeline that identifies 5 cancer types — breast, renal, lung, prostate, and colon — from RNA-seq gene expression data. After rigorous preprocessing, model selection, and hyperparameter tuning, Logistic Regression achieved a perfect F1 of 1.0, with XGBoost close behind at 0.99. Built to support earlier diagnosis and save lives.

Research · MSc Dissertation
Explainable GNN for
Fake News Detection

A Graph Attention Network trained on the UPFD/GossipCop dataset, enhanced with a Concept Bottleneck Model for intrinsic explainability. Unlike post-hoc methods, the CBM forces reasoning through human-interpretable concepts — making the model's decisions transparent by design, not as an afterthought.
